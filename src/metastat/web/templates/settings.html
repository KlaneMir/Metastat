Add Meta connection

  1. Business Manager → Business settings → Users → System users.
  2. Assign the campaigns' pages and Instagram accounts to the user.
  3. Generate token: expiry "Never", permissions {{ 'pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, read_insights, instagram_basic, instagram_manage_insights, business_management' }}.

Test account

simulator

A made-up Meta account with a dozen or so campaigns and posts. Post images are placeholders (or free photos with a "TEST" watermark, if they were downloaded). The program then talks to the simulator instead of Meta, so you can safely practise with reports.

Automatic runs

Windows Task Scheduler: a full sync every day at 6:00 and hourly readings of posts younger than 7 days from 8:00 to 22:00, for all Meta connections (test accounts are skipped). If the computer was off, the task runs right after it is turned on.
