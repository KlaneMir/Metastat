How the numbers are counted

Followers: the program's record from the last day of the month. Views and interactions: all profile activity in the month. Reactions, comments, shares: under the month's posts, as of the last reading before the month ended. Comparison: with the previous calendar month. Suspicious values are flagged, not hidden.
